STRIPE PLACEHOLDERS

Main website:
Open script.js and replace:
  premium: ''
  social: ''
  website: ''
with your three Stripe Payment Links.

Preview template:
Open preview/sample-property/index.html and replace:
  const links={premium:'',social:'',website:''};
with your Stripe Payment Links.

HOW TO CREATE A NEW PROPERTY PREVIEW

1. Duplicate the folder:
   preview/sample-property
2. Rename the duplicate to something URL-safe, e.g.:
   preview/the-sail-loft
3. Edit the property name/title in its index.html.
4. Replace the video source if using a unique preview video.
5. Upload the whole site to your hosting.

Example URL:
  https://getmyvideo.co.uk/preview/the-sail-loft/
